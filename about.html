ABOUT ME

Who I Am

I am a UI/UX Designer and Photographer based in Bali. I focus on functional, calm, and meaningful design.

In each of my works, I prioritize a balance between visual aesthetics and an intuitive user experience. My approach combines research, visual exploration, and storytelling.

Besides digital design, photography is my medium for capturing emotions, spaces, and details often overlooked.
